  • Use latitude and longitude from the in-game surface HUD.
  • Target the sun, aim at the centre of the targeted sun marker, and read the elevation value.
  • Positive altitude = above the horizon. Negative altitude = below the horizon. 0° = sunrise or sunset.
  • Only enter heading if you are pointing directly at the centre of the sun. If unsure, leave it empty.
