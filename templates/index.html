Elite Dangerous Day/Night Calculator

Predict day/night times on planets and moons in Elite Dangerous. Select a system and body, choose coordinates or a POI, and see the local sun elevation, next sunrise or sunset, sunlight duration, and a simple local sun elevation view.

How can you help?

You can help by submitting sun observations from the surface of a planet or moon.

  • Use your commander / observer name.
  • Use the current UTC time.
  • Use latitude and longitude from the in-game surface HUD.
  • Target the sun, aim at the centre of the targeted sun marker, and read the elevation value from your HUD.
  • Heading is optional. Only enter it if you are pointing directly at the centre of the sun.

Positive altitude = sun above the horizon. Negative altitude = sun below the horizon. 0° = sunrise or sunset. Observations are reviewed before they become part of the verified model.

Verified and provisional predictions

The normal prediction is based on reviewed observations. If new unreviewed observations exist, you can try a provisional model. Provisional predictions can be useful, but they may change after reviewers check the data.
