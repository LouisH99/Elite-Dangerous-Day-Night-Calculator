Observation guide

Use this guide when submitting a sun observation from a planet or moon surface. The marked HUD values are the ones the calculator needs.

Before you submit

  1. Hover over the body you want to observe.
  2. Target the local sun in-game.
  3. Aim as close as possible at the centre of the targeted sun marker.
  4. Use the live UTC time on the form, or enter the UTC time shown in-game.
  5. Copy latitude, longitude, and sun elevation from the HUD.

Reading the HUD

  • Latitude and longitude: bottom-right surface coordinates. Keep the minus signs when shown.
  • Elevation: the vertical angle to the targeted sun. Copy the signed number from the HUD as accurately as you can.
  • Heading: the top compass value. Only submit it when you are pointed directly at the sun marker.

Form fields

FieldWhat to enterNotes
Observer nameYour commander or reviewer-recognizable name.Use the same name for future submissions.

LongitudeThe second surface coordinate.Enter the HUD value as shown. Example: -23.72146.
Sun elevationThe vertical sun angle in degrees.Example: -10. Decimals such as -10.5 are okay if you are confident.
Sun headingThe compass heading in degrees.Optional. Leave empty unless aimed at the sun marker.
QualityHow accurate the reading is.Use low for rough screenshots or uncertain aiming.

Good observation habits

  • Submit more than one observation only if the time or sun position has meaningfully changed.
  • Avoid entering heading if you are not lined up with the sun marker; elevation alone is usually more reliable.
  • Add a note when visibility is poor, the sun marker is hard to read, or the value came from a screenshot.
