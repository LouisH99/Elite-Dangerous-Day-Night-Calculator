About this tool

This website predicts day/night times on planets and moons in Elite Dangerous.

Select a system and body, choose a location or point of interest, and the tool shows current sun altitude, day/night state, next sunrise or sunset, sunlight duration, and a local sun elevation view.

How predictions are made

Predictions are based on player-submitted sun observations. More good observations make the model more accurate. Submitted observations are reviewed before they become part of the verified model.

How to submit a useful observation

For a cockpit screenshot walkthrough, see the observation guide.

  1. Land on the target body.
  2. Use latitude and longitude from the in-game surface HUD.
  3. Target the sun in-game.
  4. Aim as close as possible at the centre of the targeted sun marker.
  5. Read the elevation / altitude value from your HUD.
  6. Submit the UTC time, coordinates, sun altitude, and your observer name.

Heading is optional. Only enter heading if you are pointing directly at the centre of the sun. If unsure, leave it empty.

POIs

Points of Interest are saved locations on a body. Clicking a POI immediately predicts the local day/night state for that location. You can submit new POIs for review from the top navigation.
